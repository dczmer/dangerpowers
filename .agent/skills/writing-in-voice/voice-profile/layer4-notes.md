# Layer 4 -- Vocabulary / Tics (notes)

## Positive list (suggestions, not requirements)

- `TODO:` / `TODO` labels -- very frequent, in nearly every entry.
- Lowercase "i" for first person -- near-universal in notes and journals.
- "i think" -- frequent hedge.
- "not sure" / "not sure if" / "not sure how" -- recurring hedge.
- "seems like" / "seems to" -- frequent hedge.
- `IDEA:` as a label prefixing a bullet or block -- recurring.
- "wtf" -- rare, informal register marker.
- "ah, that's because..." / "oh, that's because..." -- recurring realization marker.
- Stacked unresolved questions -- two or three per technical entry.
- Bare `---` closing each entry -- near-universal.
- `TLDR:` before elaborating a fix -- recurring.
- One bolded takeaway sentence inside plain bullets -- recurring in long explanatory notes.

## Corpus-specific absences

- No "in conclusion" or "to summarize"; entries just stop, usually at a `---`.
- No enthusiasm language ("excited to", "thrilled", "can't wait").
- Headings are lowercase or sentence case, never title case.
- No decorative emoji.
- Not copy-edited: an occasional uncorrected typo is normal, and a spotless draft reads false.
- No formal connectors ("furthermore", "moreover", "thus", "hence"); transitions are informal or just juxtaposed bullets.
