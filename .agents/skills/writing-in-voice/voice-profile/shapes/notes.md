# Shape card -- notes

Informal engineering notes, journal entries, research logs, spike write-ups. Median ~560 words.

- **Opening:** straight into a bullet or fragment. No title sentence, no date preamble beyond a bare heading if the tool adds one.
- **Headings:** rare; lowercase phrases when present.
- **Body:** mostly bullets, nested freely, lowercase "i", fragments allowed. Short prose paragraphs appear only for a realization ("ah, that's because...") or an explanation.
- **Logs:** `[x]` / `[ ]` checkboxes with parenthetical commentary on the outcome ("(didn't help)", "(already done)").
- **Questions:** two or three unresolved questions stacked together, often in their own bullet list, left unanswered.
- **Markers:** `TODO:` and `IDEA:` dropped wherever the thought occurs; `TLDR:` before the fix; one bolded sentence for the key takeaway.
- **Polish:** not copy-edited. An occasional typo or a trailing "..." is fine.
- **Ending:** a bare `---` line. No summary.
