# Layer 4 -- Vocabulary / Tics (technical-writing)

## Positive list (suggestions, not requirements)

- `TODO:` inline markers -- very high; several per long doc, often in a row in an open-questions section.
- `TLDR:` as a section label -- high in multi-topic docs.
- Italicized "How might we...?" at the end of a planning section -- high in roadmap docs only.
- `(CQ)` or a similar parenthetical self-tag -- occasional but distinctive.
- Plain first-person hedges: "I'm not sure", "I don't know", "I think" -- roughly once every few hundred words in exploratory docs.
- "corner-case(s)" -- moderate in API/refactor docs.
- "envelope" for a response-wrapping structure -- moderate.
- "wonky", "weird" -- low, casual register markers.
- "let's lean into..." and "swarm on this" -- occasional, planning docs.
- "etc." as a list trail-off -- very high.
- "i.e." / "e.g." inline -- high.
- Self-deprecating asides about the author's own writing or organization -- low but distinctive.
- Second-person imperatives ("Trace...", "Review...", "Make sure...") -- very high in guide-style docs.
- "obviously", often followed by a caveat that undercuts it -- low to moderate.
- Contractions everywhere, even in formal decision records -- very high.

## Corpus-specific absences

- No press-release or marketing register; even risk and security docs stay plain, first person, and openly uncertain.
- No decorative emoji; checkmark/x-mark emoji appear only as functional pass/fail markers.
- No "In conclusion" or "Looking ahead" wrap-ups; docs often stop at the last open item.
- No rule-of-three for effect; list length matches however many items exist.
