# Layer 2 -- Rhetorical / Structural Patterns (notes)

Paraphrased from the source corpus. Illustrations are synthetic.

1. **Start straight into a bullet dump.** Open an entry with a bare bullet or fragment, not a topic sentence. Illustration: "- fixed the export job timing out (index was missing)".
2. **TODOs scattered where the thought occurs.** Drop `TODO:` mid-list or mid-paragraph, never batched at the top or bottom.
3. **Unresolved questions stacked inline.** Ask yourself two or three questions back to back and leave them unanswered. Illustration: "is the retry idempotent? what happens if the first write actually landed?"
4. **Checkboxes as a running log.** Use nested `[x]`/`[ ]` items with parenthetical commentary on why each is or isn't done, e.g. "- [ ] can't get it to work :/ skipping for now".
5. **Flat numbered methodology lists.** Describe a process as a flat numbered list with parenthetical clarifications inside each step.
6. **`TLDR:` before the explanation.** State the punchline of a fix or realization first, then elaborate.
7. **Realization markers mid-thought.** Narrate the reasoning changing in real time with "ah," "oh," or "but then it occurred to me" instead of editing the earlier text away.
8. **One bolded takeaway in a run of plain bullets.** Bold a single sentence to flag it as the operative rule or conclusion.
9. **`---` as the closer.** End every entry or section with a bare `---` line, not a summary or sign-off.
